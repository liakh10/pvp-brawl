PLAYER VS PLAYER DOCS

Seven Robinhood Chain memes race for one glass P. Every visitor pushes in the same arena on a shared server

01 · overviewOne arena for everyone

Player vs Player is a daily race between seven meme fighters from Robinhood Chain. Pick one and push. Every push moves your fighter up the green chart toward the glass P, and every visitor on the site pushes in the same arena at the same time.

Pushes are free and have no value. They are not tokens, points you can sell or claims on anything. The $PVP token is real and separate.

03 · mechanicSmash the button

Tap the green button, or press space while the arena is on screen. Your page counts pushes instantly and sends them to the server in batches every 0.7 seconds, up to 40 per request.

├── position on the chart    fighter score ÷ leader score
├── leader                   wears the crown, stands closest to the P
├── refresh                  the page reloads the arena every 3 seconds

04 · 00:00 UTCA new race every day

Scores reset at 00:00 UTC. The fighter with the most pushes when the round ends becomes yesterday’s king and is shown on the site for the next day. All-time totals per fighter are kept on the server.

05 · identityA key in this browser

The first visit creates a random 32-character key in this browser. The server derives a public player id from it with SHA-256. Top pushers are shown by the first 6 characters of that id, your own row says you. Clearing site data starts a new player.

07 · infrastructureOne server, one state

Pushes go to a Vercel Function that increments counters in Upstash Redis: the fighter’s daily score, the all-time total, your daily count and the daily top list. Reading the arena is a single request that returns all of it.

08 · tokenThe token

09 · questionsQuestions

Do pushes earn anything?No. They decide who wears the crown today, nothing more.
Is it the same arena for everyone?Yes. Every visitor pushes into the same scores on the same server.
Why did my pushes stop?You hit the per-IP limit or lost connection. Wait a few seconds and push again.
When does the round reset?Every day at 00:00 UTC. The timer on the arena counts down to it.
